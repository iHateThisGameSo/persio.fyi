I direct AI agents to implement software. My work is defining the problem, setting priorities, challenging the proposed approach, and deciding whether the result meets the requirements.

A plausible explanation is not enough to accept a change. I ask for evidence: a working build, a test of the relevant behavior, or an observation I can check. The code can be delegated; the delivery judgment stays with me.
