My agent workflow uses structured handoffs, scoped locks, and shared decision and incident logs.

The handoff records what changed, what was checked, and what remains open. The lock gives shared work a clear owner. The logs let another session recover a decision without reconstructing an entire conversation.

These are small operational tools, but they are part of the work, alongside the code.
