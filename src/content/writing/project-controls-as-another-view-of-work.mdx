My independent work also includes project risk management and control: tracking cost, schedule, and scope, and keeping risk registers and decision logs.

AI helps with analysis and reporting. I still own the questions being asked and the judgment about the result. A useful report needs to make the next decision clearer, whether the project is a piece of software or a wider delivery effort.
